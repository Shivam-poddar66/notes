# Revision Cycles and Spaced Repetition

Last updated: February 26, 2026  
Phase: Setup and DSA Learning System (Phase 0)

## Goal
Retain solved patterns long term instead of forgetting them after one accepted submission.

## Revision Schedule
Use fixed checkpoints after first solve:
- D+1: quick review of approach and key invariant.
- D+3: re-solve from scratch with no notes.
- D+7: timed re-solve.
- D+14: mixed-topic recall test.
- D+30: final retention check.

## What to Revise
- Pattern trigger: how to recognize this problem type quickly.
- State/invariant definition.
- Common wrong approach and why it fails.
- Complexity and constraints fit.
- One minimal edge case that breaks weak solutions.

## Problem Tags for Revision Queue
- `weak`: failed or solved with heavy hints.
- `normal`: solved but not fully confident.
- `strong`: solved quickly and explained clearly.

## Weekly Revision Mix
- 60% from `weak`.
- 30% from `normal`.
- 10% from `strong` (to keep speed sharp).

## Retention Score
For each revisited problem:
- 2 points: solved correctly under time.
- 1 point: solved with minor help.
- 0 points: could not solve.

Interpretation:
- 80%+ of max score: retention is healthy.
- 60% to 79%: add more D+3 and D+7 re-solves.
- Below 60%: reduce new problems and focus on consolidation.

## Exit Criteria
- [ ] Revision queue is created and tagged.
- [ ] At least one D+1 and one D+3 cycle is completed.
- [ ] Retention score is being tracked weekly.
