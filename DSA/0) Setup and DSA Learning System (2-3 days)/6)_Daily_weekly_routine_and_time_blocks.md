# Daily Weekly Routine and Time Blocks

Last updated: February 26, 2026  
Phase: Setup and DSA Learning System (Phase 0)

## Goal
Build a routine that survives busy days and still improves problem-solving skill.

## Daily Structure (2-Hour Version)
- 15 min: revision of yesterday's mistakes.
- 60 min: timed problem solving.
- 30 min: editorial comparison and rewrite key insight.
- 15 min: update tracker and next-day plan.

## Daily Structure (4-Hour Version)
- 30 min: revision and warmup.
- 120 min: two timed problem blocks.
- 45 min: deep postmortem and template extraction.
- 25 min: re-solve one old failed problem.

## Weekly Rhythm
- 5 focused practice days.
- 1 revision-heavy day.
- 1 light day (or rest) with only tracker cleanup and quick review.

## Time-Block Rules
- Keep a fixed start time when possible.
- Use one device/window for coding to reduce context switching.
- During timed blocks, no hints for at least first 20 minutes.

## Low-Energy Backup Plan (Minimum Effective Session)
- Solve one easy or revisit one old medium.
- Update mistake log with one insight.
- Keep streak alive even if session is only 30 to 40 minutes.

## Exit Criteria
- [ ] Calendar has fixed study slots for next 2 weeks.
- [ ] You chose either 2-hour or 4-hour default plan.
- [ ] You have a backup plan for low-energy days.
