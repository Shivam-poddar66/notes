# Phase 1 Study Plan (1-2 weeks)

Last updated: February 26, 2026  
Phase: Complexity and Problem Solving Foundations (Phase 1)

## Objective
Master complexity analysis and problem-solving methodology before entering topic-heavy phases.

## Target Outcomes by End of Phase
- You can estimate feasible complexity from constraints in under 1 minute.
- You can move from brute force to optimized approach systematically.
- You can explain correctness with invariants or induction.
- You can debug using structure, not guesswork.

## Problem Volume Targets
- Core target (7-day plan): 35 to 45 problems.
- Extended target (12 to 14-day plan): 50 to 70 problems.
- Suggested difficulty mix: 40% easy, 50% medium, 10% hard.

## 7-Day Core Plan
### Day 1
- Big O, Theta, Omega fundamentals.
- 5 problems focused on complexity identification.

### Day 2
- Time-space tradeoffs.
- 5 problems with alternative approaches and comparison notes.

### Day 3
- Amortized analysis and operation-sequence reasoning.
- 4 to 5 problems with stack/window/dynamic array behavior.

### Day 4
- Recurrences and Master theorem basics.
- 5 analysis drills + 2 coding problems.

### Day 5
- Correctness proofs, invariants, and induction.
- 4 problems with written proof outline before coding.

### Day 6
- Constraint-driven selection and brute-force to optimized transitions.
- 6 timed problems, each with transformation notes.

### Day 7
- Pseudocode, dry runs, counterexamples, debugging drill.
- 5 mixed problems + full review and weak-area list.

## Extended Days (Optional, Recommended)
- Days 8 to 10: medium-heavy mixed sets with strict timing.
- Days 11 to 12: re-solve weak problems from Day 1 to Day 7.
- Days 13 to 14: mock interview blocks and final revision.

## Daily Session Template (2.5 to 4 Hours)
1. 20 min revision from previous day mistakes.
2. 90 to 150 min timed solving block.
3. 40 min editorial comparison and note refinement.
4. 20 min tracker + next-day plan.

## Weekly Score Targets
- Accuracy: 75%+ by end of week.
- Hint dependency: below 30%.
- Re-solve success (D+7 subset): 65%+.
- Median medium solve time: 35 to 45 minutes.

## Exit Criteria for Phase 1
- [ ] Completed minimum problem target.
- [ ] Maintained complexity and proof notes for each topic.
- [ ] Finished one mixed 90-minute mock with postmortem.
- [ ] Can explain final approach and complexity cleanly for medium problems.
