# Phase 2 Study Plan (2-3 weeks)

Last updated: February 26, 2026  
Phase: Arrays Strings and Hashing (Phase 2)

## Objective
Build strong medium-level problem solving in arrays, strings, and hashing with interview-ready speed and accuracy.

## Target Outcomes
- Solve most medium problems in 25 to 40 minutes.
- Recognize patterns quickly (hash, prefix, window, sort, Kadane, matrix).
- Reduce hint dependency and repeated bug classes.

## Week-by-Week Plan
### Week 1: Foundations + Core Patterns
- Arrays in-place patterns.
- String frequency and map/set basics.
- Prefix sums and counting patterns.
- Target: 18 to 22 problems.

### Week 2: Intermediate Transformations
- Sliding windows and advanced map usage.
- Matrix and 2D patterns.
- Kadane and sort-based transformations.
- Target: 18 to 22 problems.

### Week 3: Consolidation + Mock Sessions
- Collision/key design and edge-case stress.
- Timed mixed sets from all buckets.
- Re-solve weak and previously failed problems.
- Target: 20+ problems including re-solves.

## Daily Session Template (2.5 to 4 Hours)
1. 15 min revision of previous mistakes.
2. 90 to 150 min timed solving block.
3. 30 to 45 min deep postmortem and note updates.
4. 15 min planning next session.

## Weekly Metrics
- Accuracy target: 75%+.
- Hint dependency target: below 30%.
- Median medium solve time: 35 to 45 minutes.
- Re-solve success rate: 65%+ on D+7 set.

## Problem Distribution Recommendation
- 30% easy.
- 60% medium.
- 10% hard.

## Review Rules
- After every 10 problems, analyze top 3 recurring mistakes.
- After every week, do one 90-minute mixed mock.
- Re-solve weak-tagged problems before adding new hard problems.

## Exit Criteria
- [ ] At least 60 tracked problems completed.
- [ ] Pattern-wise confidence is documented.
- [ ] One final mixed mock completed with postmortem.
- [ ] Weak areas prepared for Phase 3 handoff.
