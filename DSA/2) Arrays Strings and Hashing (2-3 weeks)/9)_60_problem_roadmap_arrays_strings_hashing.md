# 60 Problem Roadmap: Arrays, Strings, Hashing

Last updated: February 26, 2026  
Phase: Arrays Strings and Hashing (Phase 2)

## Goal
Complete a balanced 60-problem set that builds pattern recognition, speed, and retention.

## Difficulty Split
- Easy: 18
- Medium: 36
- Hard: 6

## Pattern Buckets (5 Problems Each)
1. Array basics and in-place updates.
2. Frequency counting and anagram patterns.
3. Hash map complement and lookup patterns.
4. Prefix sum and subarray counting.
5. Sliding window on strings.
6. Matrix traversal and transformations.
7. Kadane and max/min contiguous segments.
8. Sorting + scan + two pointers.
9. Grouping and signature hashing.
10. Mixed review and timed drills.
11. Edge-case heavy stress set.
12. Mock interview set.

## Recommended Progression
- Week 1: Buckets 1 to 4.
- Week 2: Buckets 5 to 8.
- Week 3: Buckets 9 to 12 + consolidation.

## Session Structure Per Problem
1. 3 to 5 minutes: identify pattern and complexity target.
2. 15 to 40 minutes: solve under timer.
3. 10 minutes: test, postmortem, and note update.

## Tracking Fields (Mandatory)
- Problem ID/link.
- Pattern bucket.
- Solve status (independent / hint / unsolved).
- Time spent.
- Bug type.
- Re-solve date.

## Milestone Checks
### At 20 problems
- Accuracy 70%+.
- Basic map/set and prefix patterns stable.

### At 40 problems
- Medium problems mostly solvable without hints.
- Average medium solve time under 45 minutes.

### At 60 problems
- 75%+ first-attempt correctness on review set.
- Strong recall on D+7 and D+30 re-solves.

## Retention Rule
- Re-solve at least 12 problems from the 60:
  - 6 at D+7
  - 6 at D+30

## Exit Criteria
- [ ] 60 problems completed with tracker entries.
- [ ] All 12 buckets covered.
- [ ] Weak-bucket list created for next revision cycle.
