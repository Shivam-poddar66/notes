# 5) Chapter 0 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **Identity Shift**: Transition from direct individual contributor output ($1x$) to organizational force multiplication ($10x+$). Stop chasing compiler dopamine and embrace human enablement.
- **Avoid the 3 Traps**: Do not become the *Hero Coder* (hoarding hard code), the *Micromanager* (smothering autonomy), or the *Abdicator* (abandoning direction).
- **Situational Leadership II**: Diagnose each engineer's task-specific development level (D1 to D4) and modulate your style accordingly between Directing (S1), Coaching (S2), Supporting (S3), and Delegating (S4).
- **Andy Grove’s Output Equation**: Focus your time strictly on high-leverage activities—initiatives that positively affect many people over a long duration.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Keyboard Detachment**: I no longer measure my daily productivity by lines of code written or tickets closed personally.
2. [ ] **Zero Hero Coding**: When deadlines crunch, I do not take critical path coding tasks onto myself.
3. [ ] **Task-Specific Diagnosis**: I evaluate an engineer's competence per specific task rather than assuming a general blanket skill level.
4. [ ] **Directive Support for Beginners**: I provide clear step-by-step guidance to junior engineers (S1) without assuming they will figure it out alone.
5. [ ] **True Delegation for Seniors**: I give senior engineers high autonomy over implementation details (S4) once goals are agreed upon.
6. [ ] **High-Leverage Prioritization**: I spend at least 60% of my weekly time on high-leverage activities (training, unblocking, 1-on-1s, strategy).
7. [ ] **Negative Leverage Elimination**: I actively cancel low-value meetings and avoid impulsive, panicky reactions that distract the team.
8. [ ] **Emotional Regulation**: I absorb ambiguity and pressure from leadership rather than amplifying it down onto my team.
9. [ ] **Psychological Reframing**: I find deep satisfaction in watching my reports get promoted and recognized for technical victories.
10. [ ] **Calendar Alignment**: My calendar directly reflects my team's top 3 strategic priorities, not reactive firefighting.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Fully transformed into an executive force multiplier.
- **11–16**: Good leadership foundation; occasional slip-ups into hero coding during crises.
- **0–10**: Stuck in the IC identity crisis; step back from code immediately and focus on managerial leverage.
