# 5) Chapter 7 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **The Triad (Product, Design, Engineering)**: Maintain explicit ownership lines (PM owns the Why/Value, Design owns the Experience, Engineering owns the Architecture/Feasibility). Involve engineers in early customer discovery (Shift Left).
- **Cross-Functional Fluency**: Speak the language of Sales (revenue enablement), Marketing (positioning), and Finance (gross margin and cloud unit economics).
- **The "No Surprises" Rule**: Deliver bad news early with the speed of light. Never allow an executive leader to be blindsided by system outages or slipping milestones.
- **Executive Sponsorship**: Understand the difference between mentors (who talk to you) and sponsors (who use political capital to advocate for your promotion in closed rooms). Earn sponsorship by solving leaders' highest-stakes problems.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Triad Harmony**: My team operates in close lockstep with our Product Manager and Product Designer with zero territorial infighting.
2. [ ] **Early Customer Discovery**: Engineers on my team participate in user interviews and understand real customer pain points.
3. [ ] **Commercial Empathy**: I respect and collaborate effectively with Sales, Marketing, and Customer Support teams.
4. [ ] **Cloud Unit Economics**: I track and manage our team's AWS/GCP cloud spend with the fiduciary rigor of a CFO.
5. [ ] **"No Surprises" Discipline**: My manager never hears about a major outage, missed sprint, or resignation from someone else first.
6. [ ] **Executive Escalation Format**: When flagging risks to leadership, I lead with the bottom line (BLUF), business impact, and 2 concrete options.
7. [ ] **Autonomous Problem Ownership**: I consistently bring solutions and recommendations to my manager rather than dropping problems on their desk.
8. [ ] **Executive Alignment**: I understand the top 3 existential risks keeping my VP or CTO awake at night.
9. [ ] **Building Sponsors**: I have cultivated trusted relationships with senior leaders who actively advocate for my team in closed rooms.
10. [ ] **Generous Credit Distribution**: When my team achieves a high-visibility victory, I publicly highlight my engineers and share credit across teams.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Elite cross-functional leader and indispensable executive partner.
- **11–16**: Strong operational management; occasional blind spots regarding commercial or executive visibility.
- **0–10**: Siloed engineering mindset, poor executive communication, and high vulnerability to surprise crises; apply the "No Surprises Rule" immediately.
