# 5) Chapter 9 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **Executive Presence**: Balance Power (status and decisiveness) with Warmth (empathy and inclusion). Master physical stillness and eliminate restless fidgeting in the boardroom.
- **Boardroom Metrics**: Speak the financial language of capital allocators: Gross Margin %, R&D Expense Ratio (15–25% of ARR), Hosting COGS, and DORA cycle time metrics.
- **Cross-Organizational Coalitions**: Never pitch major technical transformations cold. Build informal coalitions with Product, Sales, and Finance 1-on-1 before formal executive committee meetings.
- **High-Status Vocalics**: Eradicate minimizing language (*"just", "sorry", "maybe"*). Use deep chest resonance, measured cadence (120 WPM), strategic pauses, and decisive downward inflections.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Physical Stillness**: In executive meetings, my head and hands remain still; I do not fidget, pace, or nod like a bobblehead.
2. [ ] **Power & Warmth Balance**: I can deliver decisive, firm technical decisions while maintaining authentic interpersonal warmth.
3. [ ] **Financial Fluency**: I can explain how our engineering architecture directly impacts Gross Margin and hosting COGS.
4. [ ] **R&D Capital Stewardship**: I track our engineering department's R&D spend as a percentage of overall company revenue.
5. [ ] **DORA Metrics Rigor**: I measure our organization’s deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate, and MTTR.
6. [ ] **Informal Coalition Building**: I pre-align major technical proposals with key cross-functional leaders before formal meetings.
7. [ ] **Elimination of Minimizers**: I do not use phrases like *"Sorry to bother you"* or *"I just think maybe"* in leadership communication.
8. [ ] **Downward Inflection**: I end executive recommendations with a firm descending pitch rather than questioning upspeak.
9. [ ] **Strategic Silence**: When asked difficult questions by executives, I pause calmly for 2 seconds before answering.
10. [ ] **Fiduciary Mindset**: The Board and executive committee view me as a trusted business partner who protects company capital.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Elite executive presence and boardroom governance capability.
- **11–16**: Strong leadership presence; occasional slip into engineer jargon when presenting to non-technical executives.
- **0–10**: Seen as purely a technical taskmaster rather than an executive business partner; study financial metrics and vocalics immediately.
