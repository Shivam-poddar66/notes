# 6) Chapter 10 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **1-on-1 Canvas**: Track weekly human well-being, strategic feedback, career progression, and closed-loop action items in a shared private document.
- **Promotion Readiness Rubric**: Base promotions strictly on sustained performance at the next level for at least 3–6 months. Compile objective evidence packets (RFCs, metrics, 360 peer feedback).
- **Delegation & RACI Worksheet**: Eliminate ambiguous ownership by assigning exactly one Accountable individual per workstream and defining explicit delegation levels (1 to 5).
- **Incident Commander Runbook**: Maintain calm and control during P0 outages by separating operations, communications, and scribe roles, prioritizing mitigation over root cause.
- **100-Point Audit Rubric**: Systematically quantify your leadership capabilities across 10 empirical categories to guide your growth from Frontline Manager to VP/CTO.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **1-on-1 Canvas Discipline**: I maintain an ongoing collaborative agenda document with each direct report.
2. [ ] **Action Item Tracking**: We review past commitments in the first 2 minutes of our 1-on-1s.
3. [ ] **Objective Promotion Packets**: My promotion nominations feature verified project artifacts, RFCs, and cross-functional feedback.
4. [ ] **No Premature Promotions**: I only promote engineers who have already demonstrated sustained success at the next level.
5. [ ] **RACI Discipline**: Every major initiative launched has a completed RACI worksheet with a single Accountable leader.
6. [ ] **Emergency IC Runbook**: My organization has a written Incident Commander runbook accessible to all on-call engineers.
7. [ ] **Mitigation First Culture**: During outages, our engineers instinctively prioritize rollbacks and traffic shedding over debugging.
8. [ ] **Quarterly Leadership Self-Audit**: I evaluate my management performance using the 100-Point Rubric every quarter.
9. [ ] **Target Tier Consistency**: My leadership evaluation consistently scores 85+ points on the Master Rubric.
10. [ ] **Continuous Leadership Growth**: I actively solicit 360 feedback from reports, peers, and executives to identify my blind spots.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Master of operational execution, team empowerment, and technical leadership.
- **11–16**: Strong management practices; occasional gaps in formal tooling or promotion documentation.
- **0–10**: Ad-hoc, unstructured management; adopt the canvases and checklists immediately to bring rigor to your practice.
