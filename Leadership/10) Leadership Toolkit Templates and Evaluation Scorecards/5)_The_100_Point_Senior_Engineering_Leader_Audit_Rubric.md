# 5) The 100-Point Senior Engineering Leader Audit Rubric

## Executive Overview
True leadership excellence is measurable. This comprehensive 100-point rubric provides an analytical scorecard for evaluating engineering managers, directors, and VPs of engineering across 10 critical operational, architectural, and interpersonal dimensions.

---

## 2. Seniority Scoring Bands & Readiness Tiers

```text
  [ 90 – 100 POINTS ]: TIER 1 VP OF ENGINEERING / CTO READINESS
  • Transformational organizational architect; commands boardrooms;
    builds autonomous multi-tier leadership structures; fiduciary mindset.

  [ 75 – 89 POINTS ]: TIER 2 DIRECTOR OF ENGINEERING
  • High-leverage strategic leader; manages managers effectively;
    balances multi-team topologies, hiring loops, and cross-functional alignment.

  [ 60 – 74 POINTS ]: TIER 3 COMPETENT FRONTLINE MANAGER
  • Solid operational execution; occasional slips into hero coding,
    tactical 1-on-1s, or hesitation around difficult performance conversations.

  [ BELOW 60 POINTS ]: TIER 4 STRUGGLING / IC IDENTITY TRAP
  • Bottlenecking technical deliverables; micro-managing or abdicating;
    chronic canceled 1-on-1s; high team turnover and developer burnout.
```
