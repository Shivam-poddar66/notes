# 1) The Weekly 1-on-1 Preparation Canvas

## Executive Overview
A 1-on-1 meeting without preparation quickly degrades into an awkward silence or an aimless chat about weekend plans. This practical 1-page canvas provides a structured template for managers and engineers to maintain continuity, track action items, and ensure balanced focus across human well-being, strategic feedback, and long-term career growth.

---

## 1. The 1-on-1 Canvas Template

```text
================================================================================
                     WEEKLY 1-ON-1 PREPARATION CANVAS
================================================================================
ENGINEER: Jane Doe (Senior Backend Eng)     DATE & TIME: Tuesday, 10:00 AM
MANAGER: Alex Chen (Engineering Manager)    CADENCE: Weekly (30 Mins)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HUMAN CONNECTION & WELL-BEING (10 MINS - REPORT'S AGENDA)
   • How are you feeling this week? (Energy level: 1 to 10):
   • What was your biggest operational frustration or blocker this week?
   • Personal / Life updates (if they choose to share):
   • Notes from Report:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STRATEGIC CONTEXT & REAL-TIME FEEDBACK (10 MINS - MANAGER'S AGENDA)
   • Specific Praise (SBI Model):
     "Loved how you handled the database deadlock in yesterday's release..."
   • Constructive Coaching / Course Correction:
     "In the RFC review, make sure to include an alternatives-considered matrix..."
   • Organizational News / Strategic Updates:
     "Heads up on our Q3 European data center expansion bet..."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CAREER GROWTH & SKILL ACQUISITION (10 MINS - FUTURE AGENDA)
   • Promotion Roadmap Milestone Status:
     [ ] Led system design RFC    [ ] Mentored junior hire    [ ] On-call lead
   • What technical or leadership skill do you want to practice this sprint?
   • What high-visibility project can we pair you with next quarter?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CLOSED-LOOP ACTION ITEMS & COMMITMENTS
   • Action Item 1 (Report): Schedule architecture review with DBA by Thursday.
   • Action Item 2 (Manager): Secure budget approval for new Datadog APM licenses.
   • Next 1-on-1 Date: Next Tuesday, 10:00 AM (Uncancelable).
================================================================================
```

---

## 2. Guidelines for Using the Canvas
- Share this canvas in a shared, private collaborative document (e.g. Google Docs, Notion, or internal wiki).
- Both manager and report can add bullet points asynchronously throughout the week as thoughts arise.
- Review past action items in the first 2 minutes of each meeting to enforce accountability.
