# 5) Chapter 8 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **Incident Commander System**: During major production outages, assign a dedicated IC who does not write code, prioritizes customer mitigation over root cause, and enforces single-voice radio discipline.
- **Kotter's 8-Step Change**: Drive technical migrations and agile shifts by creating urgency, forming a guiding coalition of tech leads, generating 30-day short-term wins, and anchoring new habits in culture.
- **Navigating Layoffs with Dignity**: Deliver separation news with compassion, clarity, and zero corporate euphemisms. Re-anchor remaining teams immediately by defining what projects the company will *stop* doing.
- **Burnout Prevention**: Detect exhaustion, cynicism, and ineffectiveness early. Fix painful on-call alert fatigue, establish focus blocks, and protect true vacation detachment.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Incident Command Protocol**: We have a documented Incident Commander process where the IC directs traffic without debugging code.
2. [ ] **Mitigation First**: During outages, our primary goal is immediate customer relief (rollbacks, traffic shedding) before root-cause analysis.
3. [ ] **Executive Shielding**: During incidents, engineering leads shield developers from executive pings via designated status updates.
4. [ ] **Guiding Coalitions**: When driving major architectural shifts, I partner with respected senior engineers before mandating policy.
5. [ ] **Short-Term Wins**: My change initiatives deliver visible, measurable improvements within the first 30 days.
6. [ ] **Humane Re-Orgs**: During restructuring or layoffs, I treat departing personnel with deep empathy and transparent communication.
7. [ ] **Survivor Re-Anchoring**: Following a re-org, I explicitly clarify which projects are canceled to prevent team overwork.
8. [ ] **On-Call Fatigue Tracking**: We track on-call paging frequency and immediately remediate alerts if pages exceed 3 per week.
9. [ ] **Deep Work Protection**: My team has dedicated no-meeting blocks or focus days to protect uninterrupted coding time.
10. [ ] **Vacation Detachment**: Team members completely disconnect from Slack and email during paid time off without guilt.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Elite crisis leader, resilient team culture, and change management master.
- **11–16**: Good crisis response; occasional vulnerability to team burnout during prolonged crunch periods.
- **0–10**: Chaotic outage firefighting and severe developer burnout; institutionalize the Incident Commander protocol immediately.
