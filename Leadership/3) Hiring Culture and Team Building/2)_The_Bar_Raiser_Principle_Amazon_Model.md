# 2) The Bar Raiser Principle (Amazon Model)

## Executive Overview
Pioneered by Amazon in the late 1990s, the **Bar Raiser Program** is a talent governance mechanism designed to counteract the natural human tendency for hiring standards to degrade over time. When an engineering manager is short-staffed and facing an impossible delivery deadline, they experience intense psychological pressure to lower the hiring bar just to get "a warm body in the seat". The Bar Raiser acts as an independent, objective guardian of talent quality.

---

## 1. The Core Bar Raiser Axiom

$$\text{Hiring Standard} = \text{Candidate must be better than 50% of the current employees at that level.}$$

```text
  TRADITIONAL HIRING (DEGRADATION CURVE)         THE BAR RAISER GOVERNANCE
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐        ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Hiring Manager is desperate          │        │ Independent Bar Raiser from external │
│ Lowers standards to fill head-count  │ ─────► │ team holds absolute VETO power       │
│ Average team talent declines         │        │ Standard remains high forever        │
│ High performers quit in frustration  │        │ Only top 50th percentile hired       │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘        └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. The 3 Powers of a Bar Raiser

1. **Complete Organizational Independence**:
   - The Bar Raiser belongs to a completely different department or engineering org.
   - They have zero incentive to rush a hire to meet a local sprint deadline.
2. **Absolute Veto Power**:
   - Even if the Hiring Manager desperately wants to extend an offer, the Bar Raiser can unilaterally veto the hire if the candidate does not meet the bar.
3. **Debrief Facilitation & Evidence Grounding**:
   - In the hiring debrief, the Bar Raiser forces interviewers to justify scores with direct quotes and evidence, stripping out cognitive bias, halo effects, and gut-feel assumptions.

---

## 3. How to Institute a Bar Raiser Program in Any Tech Org
- Select 5–10 of your most culturally grounded, intellectually rigorous senior engineers and managers.
- Train them on behavioral interviewing, cognitive bias detection, and debrief moderation.
- Mandate that **no hiring offer can be extended without an approved Bar Raiser sign-off**.
- This single policy prevents the gradual dilution of engineering talent as a company scales from 50 to 500+ engineers.
