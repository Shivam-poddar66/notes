# 5) Chapter 3 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **Structured Interview Loops**: Ban unstructured vibe interviews and brainteasers. Standardize on 4-pillar loops with objective, 4-point competency rubrics.
- **The Bar Raiser Principle**: Institute an independent interviewer from an outside department armed with veto power to ensure every hire is better than 50% of the current team.
- **Psychological Safety (Google Project Aristotle)**: High performance requires the combination of high accountability and high safety. Model vulnerability, run blameless post-mortems, and reward messengers of bad news.
- **Engineered Onboarding**: Measure the Day-1 Deploy milestone; structure the first 90 days with clear 30-60-90 day benchmarks; assign a dedicated onboarding buddy with protected sprint capacity.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Objective Interview Rubrics**: My team evaluates candidates against concrete written scoring rubrics rather than subjective impressions.
2. [ ] **No Puzzle Trivia**: Our technical screens focus on practical systems, architecture, and clean code rather than algorithmic trivia.
3. [ ] **Independent Feedback**: Interviewers submit written scores prior to speaking with other interviewers to prevent groupthink.
4. [ ] **Bar Raiser Sign-off**: We enforce an independent Bar Raiser review with veto power before extending any offer.
5. [ ] **Leader Vulnerability**: As a leader, I openly admit my own technical mistakes and gaps in team meetings.
6. [ ] **Blameless Incident Reviews**: In retrospectives, we examine system guards and CI/CD pipelines rather than pointing fingers at individuals.
7. [ ] **Bad News Welcomed**: When an engineer surfaces an impending risk or delay, I thank them publicly rather than expressing anger.
8. [ ] **Day-1 Deploy Capability**: A newly hired engineer can successfully deploy a change to staging/production on their very first day.
9. [ ] **Dedicated Onboarding Buddy**: Every new hire is paired with a designated buddy whose capacity is adjusted to support onboarding.
10. [ ] **Clear 30-60-90 Day Milestones**: Every new hire receives an explicit written roadmap outlining success expectations for their first 90 days.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: High-caliber hiring and psychological safety culture.
- **11–16**: Good hiring rigor; occasional onboarding friction or inconsistent rubric usage.
- **0–10**: Severe hiring degradation and fear-based culture; implement structured loops and the Bar Raiser immediately.
