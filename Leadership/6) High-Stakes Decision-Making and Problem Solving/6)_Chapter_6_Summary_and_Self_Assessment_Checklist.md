# 6) Chapter 6 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **Type 1 vs Type 2 Decisions**: Treat reversible Type 2 decisions with speed and ~70% information. Reserve slow, exhaustive analysis strictly for irreversible Type 1 one-way doors.
- **The Cynefin Framework**: Adapt your decision methodology to the operating domain: Apply SOPs in Clear, expert analysis in Complicated, safe-to-fail probes in Complex, and immediate emergency stabilization in Chaotic.
- **The Pre-Mortem Protocol**: Legitimizes and extracts hidden dissent by assuming the project has catastrophically failed 12 months in the future.
- **Second-Order Thinking**: Never evaluate an engineering policy or metric by its immediate first-order result; anticipate unintended feedback loops and perverse incentives.
- **Disagree and Commit**: Welcome vigorous intellectual debate before the call, followed by 100% unified commitment once the decision is made.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Type 2 Velocity**: I empower team members to make reversible decisions quickly without waiting for committee approval.
2. [ ] **70% Data Discipline**: On reversible tasks, I make the call with 70% of the data rather than succumbing to analysis paralysis.
3. [ ] **Irreversible Deliberation**: On Type 1 decisions (architecture rewrite, vendor lock-in), I mandate exhaustive trade-off analyses.
4. [ ] **Cynefin Awareness**: I recognize when a problem is Complex and use small experiments/canaries rather than rigid multi-month plans.
5. [ ] **Pre-Mortem Regularity**: I run a formal Pre-Mortem brainstorming session before kicking off any mission-critical system launch.
6. [ ] **Second-Order Anticipation**: When proposing new team metrics or engineering policies, I analyze how developers might game the incentives.
7. [ ] **Encouraging Disagreement**: During architecture reviews, I actively solicit contrary opinions before revealing my personal preference.
8. [ ] **Decisive Resolution**: When debates hit a deadlock, I step in and make a clear, definitive call rather than letting debate drag on.
9. [ ] **Total Commitment**: Once a decision is made, I and my team execute with 100% effort, even if our personal option was rejected.
10. [ ] **Zero Passive Sabotage**: I immediately call out and address passive-aggressive griping or foot-dragging after a decision is finalized.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Master of high-stakes technical decision-making and strategic speed.
- **11–16**: Strong decision framework; occasional tendency to let reversible decisions linger too long.
- **0–10**: Frequent bureaucratic paralysis or reckless Type 1 blunders; institutionalize the Pre-Mortem and Type 1/2 models immediately.
