# 5) Humane Offboarding and Separations

## Executive Overview
Letting someone go is the single hardest task in leadership. How a manager conducts a termination defines their character and profoundly impacts organizational culture. The remaining team members watch closely: if an employee is treated like disposable trash or escorted out by security like a criminal without dignity, psychological safety evaporates across the entire company. A leader must execute terminations with **profound clarity, professional compassion, and unshakeable respect**.

---

## 1. The Separation Protocol

```text
               ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
               │         THE TERMINATION CONVERSATION         │
               │   • Duration: 5 to 10 Minutes MAXIMUM        │
               │   • Attendees: Manager + HR Partner          │
               │   • Tone: Calm, empathetic, direct, firm     │
               └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                      │
            ┌─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┐
            ▼                                                   ▼
     WHAT TO DO                                          WHAT NEVER TO DO
  • Deliver decision in first 30 seconds              • Small talk or weather banter
  • State decision is final                           • Apologize or debate past metrics
  • Cover severance, healthcare, transition           • Blame higher management ("It's not me")
  • Wish them genuine future success                  • Get defensive or argumentative
```

---

## 2. Word-for-Word Exit Script

- **Opening (First 30 Seconds)**:
  - *"Thank you for meeting with us today, David. As you know, over the last 30 days we have been reviewing your milestones on the Performance Improvement Plan. Unfortunately, the objective benchmarks were not met, and today is your last day at Acme Corp."*
- **Silence (2 Seconds)**:
  - Let the words sink in. Do not rush to fill the silence.
- **Handling Pushback / Debate**:
  - *Employee*: *"This isn't fair. The QA environment was down for two days!"*
  - *Manager*: *"David, I understand how frustrating this is, but this decision has been fully reviewed and is final. Today we want to focus entirely on supporting your transition, your severance package, and your next steps."*
- **Handover to HR**:
  - *"Sarah from HR is here to walk through your severance benefits, health insurance continuation, and equipment return."*

---

## 3. Post-Separation Team Communication (The Same Day)

Within 2 hours of the departure, hold an immediate 10-minute huddle with the immediate team:
- **Be Respectful & Discreet**: Never disclose the private performance details of the departure (*"David is no longer with Acme Corp. We are grateful for his contributions to the platform and wish him the best in his next endeavor."*).
- **Address Immediate Workload**: Reassign critical in-flight pull requests and on-call rotations so the remaining team members don't feel dumped on.
- **Hold Space**: Allow team members to ask questions or express feelings in private 1-on-1s.
