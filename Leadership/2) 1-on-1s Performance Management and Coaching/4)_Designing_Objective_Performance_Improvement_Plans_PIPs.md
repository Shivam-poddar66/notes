# 4) Designing Objective Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

## Executive Overview
A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is often cynically viewed as mere legal paperwork preceding a termination. While a PIP must satisfy legal standards, an ethical, high-caliber engineering manager uses a PIP as a **genuine, structured bridge back to high performance**, while simultaneously establishing objective, binary documentation if separation becomes inevitable.

---

## 1. Prerequisites Before Initiating a PIP

```text
  [ THE "NO SURPRISES" PRE-FLIGHT CHECKLIST ]
  1. Has the employee received explicit verbal feedback on these gaps?
  2. Is there documented evidence of feedback in 1-on-1 notes for at least 4-6 weeks?
  3. Were they provided with the necessary tools, access, and coaching to succeed?
  4. Is the underperformance chronic and persistent, rather than a temporary personal crisis?
  
  IF ALL 4 ARE YES ──► INITIATE FORMAL 30-TO-60 DAY PIP
```

---

## 2. Anatomy of a Legally Sound & Objective PIP

A PIP must never contain vague, subjective language (*"improve communication"*, *"show more ownership"*, *"write cleaner code"*). It must contain **binary, verifiable deliverables**.

```text
================================================================================
                    PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN (PIP)
================================================================================
EMPLOYEE: Senior Backend Engineer          DURATION: 30 Calendar Days
MANAGER: Engineering Manager               REVIEW CADENCE: Weekly

1. SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE GAPS (OBSERVABLE FACTS)
   • Missed committed sprint deliverables in 3 of the last 4 sprints without notice.
   • Production regression rate: 5 critical bugs introduced over the last 60 days.
   • Code review turnaround time averaged 4.2 days vs team SLA of 24 hours.

2. OBJECTIVE SUCCESS CRITERIA (BINARY PASS / FAIL)
   • Deliver the OAuth2 migration module with > 85% test coverage by Day 21.
   • Complete assigned code reviews within the 24-hour team SLA for the next 30 days.
   • Zero P1/P2 production incidents introduced by code authored during this period.

3. SUPPORT & RESOURCES PROVIDED
   • Weekly dedicated 45-minute coaching sessions with the Engineering Manager.
   • Paired programming partner (Staff Engineer) assigned for architecture reviews.
   • Temporary relief from secondary on-call support rotation.

4. WEEKLY CHECK-IN SCHEDULE & MILESTONES
   • Day 7 Review: RFC & test plan approval.
   • Day 14 Review: Staging implementation & code review SLA audit.
   • Day 21 Review: Integration testing & automated CI verification.
   • Day 30 Final Evaluation: Formal pass / fail decision.

5. POTENTIAL OUTCOME
   Successful completion restores employee to good standing. Failure to meet all
   objective criteria will result in immediate termination of employment.
================================================================================
```

---

## 3. Managerial Mindset During a PIP
- **Do not root for failure**: Give the employee every resource to succeed. Celebrate real milestone victories.
- **Do not waver on standards**: Lowering the bar during a PIP to avoid conflict hurts team morale and signals to high performers that mediocrity is tolerated.
