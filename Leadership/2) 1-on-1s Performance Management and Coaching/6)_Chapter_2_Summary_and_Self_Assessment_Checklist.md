# 6) Chapter 2 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **The Sacred 1-on-1**: Dedicated, uncancelable weekly 30-minute meetings owned by the report. Banish status updates; focus on blockers, feedback, and career growth.
- **The GROW Model**: Ask powerful questions (Goal $\longrightarrow$ Reality $\longrightarrow$ Options $\longrightarrow$ Will) to guide engineers to solve their own technical and cross-team problems.
- **Continuous Feedback**: Praise in public, criticize in private. Deliver feedback within 48 hours using the Situation-Behavior-Impact (SBI) framework.
- **Objective PIPs**: Enforce the "No Surprises Rule". Draft binary, measurable pass/fail criteria with weekly milestone check-ins.
- **Humane Offboarding**: Execute separations in 5–10 minutes with compassion and finality. Support the departing engineer's transition while protecting team safety.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Uninterrupted 1-on-1s**: I never cancel 1-on-1s; if an emergency occurs, I reschedule within 48 hours.
2. [ ] **No Status Updates**: My 1-on-1s are completely free of tactical ticket status inquiries.
3. [ ] **GROW Coaching Discipline**: I ask questions to guide problem-solving rather than instantly telling engineers what to do.
4. [ ] **48-Hour Feedback SLA**: I deliver constructive feedback within 48 hours of an observed event.
5. [ ] **Private Critique**: I never reprimand, critique, or question an engineer's competence in front of their peers.
6. [ ] **Specific Praise**: My public praise highlights exact architectural or behavioral actions rather than vague compliments.
7. [ ] **The "No Surprises" Rule**: No engineer is ever surprised by their performance rating or review score.
8. [ ] **Objective PIP Design**: My performance plans feature binary, verifiable deliverables rather than subjective impressions.
9. [ ] **Dignified Separations**: I handle offboarding conversations with calm compassion, brevity, and zero emotional arguments.
10. [ ] **Team Re-anchoring**: Following an employee departure, I immediately communicate with the team and rebalance workloads.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Master of human enablement, coaching, and performance management.
- **11–16**: Strong people management foundation; occasional slips into status interrogations during busy weeks.
- **0–10**: Critical management failure modes (canceling 1-on-1s, delayed feedback, subjective PIPs); overhaul 1-on-1 systems immediately.
