# 6) Chapter 5 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **Hook in 60 Seconds**: Eliminate throat-clearing pleasantries. Open with a provocative question, counter-intuitive axiom, dramatic micro-story, shocking metric, or loaded dramatic silence.
- **Anti-Bullet Slide Architecture**: Never use bullet points. Replace them with 3-card columns, big number anchors, high-contrast diagrams, or full-bleed visuals.
- **The 3-Second Rule**: Slides must be comprehended within 3 seconds so attention returns to the speaker. Maintain a 24pt minimum font floor (30pt+ preferred).
- **LARA Q&A Defense**: Handle tough inquiries with Listen $\longrightarrow$ Acknowledge $\longrightarrow$ Respond (30–45s PREP) $\longrightarrow$ Anchor.
- **Strong Landings**: End presentations with a full-circle callback, the vision of the New Bliss, and an explicit, measurable Call to Action.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **No Throat Clearing**: I dive directly into my core hook within the first 15 seconds without pleasantries.
2. [ ] **Master Hook Selection**: My opening uses one of the 5 proven archetypes (inquiry, counter-intuitive truth, story, metric, silence).
3. [ ] **Zero Bullet Points**: My presentation decks are completely free of traditional nested bullet lists.
4. [ ] **Visual Layout Diversity**: I use 3-card layouts, bold stat callouts, or clean architectural diagrams instead of text blocks.
5. [ ] **3-Second Comprehension**: Every slide in my deck can be fully understood in 3 seconds or less.
6. [ ] **Typography Floor**: No font on any of my slides falls below the 24pt minimum limit.
7. [ ] **Guy Kawasaki Pacing**: My formal presentations adhere to concise slide counts and finish with generous time for Q&A.
8. [ ] **LARA Q&A Fluency**: During Q&A, I listen without interrupting, acknowledge the premise, and answer in under 45 seconds.
9. [ ] **Full-Circle Callback**: My conclusion references the opening story or question to provide structural closure.
10. [ ] **Definitive Closing Line**: I finish with a decisive downward-inflection statement and clear CTA rather than fading out.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Master keynote stagecraft; captivating slides and commanding Q&A defense.
- **11–16**: Strong presentation instincts; occasional slide clutter or rushed conclusions.
- **0–10**: Heavy reliance on text-heavy bullet slides and weak endings; overhaul slide decks using the anti-bullet rules.
