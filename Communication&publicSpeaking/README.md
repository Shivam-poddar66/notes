## 🚀 Daily Training Routine

1. **Morning Vocal & Articulation Workout (5 Mins)**:
   - 3 cycles of Huberman Double Physiological Sigh.
   - 2 minutes of the Cork-in-Mouth reading drill for consonant crispness.
2. **Impromptu Sprint (5 Mins)**:
   - Pick a random topic and deliver a 60-second talk using **PREP** or **What? So What? Now What?** without a single filler word.
3. **Weekly 3-Way Video Self-Audit**:
   - Record a 3-minute talk. Review Pass 1 (Video muted for body language), Pass 2 (Screen off for acoustic and vocal delivery), and Pass 3 (Combined for structure and persuasion).
