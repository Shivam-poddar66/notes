# 6) Chapter 7 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **Radical Candor**: Care personally while challenging directly. Avoid ruinous empathy (sugarcoating that halts growth) and obnoxious aggression (public humiliation).
- **Nonviolent Communication (NVC)**: Separate objective observations from subjective interpretations. De-escalate conflict using Observation $\longrightarrow$ Feeling $\longrightarrow$ Need $\longrightarrow$ Request.
- **Tactical Empathy & Chris Voss Negotiation**: Mirror key words to prompt elaboration, label unexpressed emotions (*"It seems like..."*), and run accusation audits to neutralize objections before they arise.
- **Crucial Conversations**: When stakes are high and emotions surge, rebuild psychological safety using Contrasting (*Don't/Do*) and the STATE model.
- **Salary & Promotion Negotiation**: Never surprise managers with compensation demands; establish a 6-month roadmap with objective milestones. Use calibrated *"How"* and *"What"* questions to negotiate value without hostility.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Direct Feedback Delivery**: I can deliver tough constructive feedback within 48 hours without avoiding the conversation.
2. [ ] **Praise in Public, Critique in Private**: I never humiliate colleagues or air dirty laundry in public channels.
3. [ ] **NVC Observation Discipline**: When frustrated, I state objective, measurable facts rather than emotional judgments.
4. [ ] **Need-Strategy Separation**: In architectural disputes, I look for the underlying shared need before fighting over strategies.
5. [ ] **Mirroring Fluency**: I instinctively repeat the last 1–3 words of a counterpart with an inquisitive upward tone to unlock information.
6. [ ] **Emotional Labeling**: I can defuse a tense colleague by verbalizing their feelings (*"It sounds like you're feeling overwhelmed..."*).
7. [ ] **Accusation Audit Preparation**: Before presenting difficult news, I list out and state the counterpart's worst assumptions first.
8. [ ] **Contrasting in Safety**: When misunderstood, I clarify what I *do not* mean before stating what I *do* mean.
9. [ ] **Promotion Roadmap Proactivity**: I define promotion milestones with my manager 6 months prior to review cycles.
10. [ ] **Calibrated "How" Questions**: In negotiations, I use open-ended *"How am I supposed to do that?"* questions instead of flat refusals.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Master negotiator, conflict resolver, and high-trust organizational leader.
- **11–16**: Solid interpersonal instincts; occasional hesitation during high-stakes emotional confrontation.
- **0–10**: Frequent conflict avoidance (ruinous empathy) or aggressive clashes; practice NVC scripts daily.
