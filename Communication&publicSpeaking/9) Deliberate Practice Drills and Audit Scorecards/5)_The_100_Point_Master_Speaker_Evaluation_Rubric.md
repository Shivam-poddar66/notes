# 5) The 100-Point Master Speaker Evaluation Rubric

## Executive Overview
Objective improvement requires precise measurement. This 100-point rubric provides an analytical, reproducible grading scorecard for evaluating recorded presentations, conference rehearsals, and executive briefings across 10 critical dimensions.

---

## 1. The 100-Point Master Evaluation Scorecard

| Dimension | Evaluation Criteria & Benchmarks | Max Pts | Your Score |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **1. Hook & Opening** | Eliminates throat-clearing pleasantries; captures total room attention in the first 30 seconds using one of the 5 master hooks. | 10 | |
| **2. Vocal Clarity & Silence** | Zero filler words (*um, uh, like*); uses intentional 2-second silent pauses to frame key insights rather than rushing. | 10 | |
| **3. Vocal Variety & Cadence** | Modulates pace (100–180 WPM) dynamically; projects deep chest resonance; avoids robotic monotone. | 10 | |
| **4. Downward Inflection** | Decisively drops vocal pitch at the end of declarative statements; completely free of insecure upspeak. | 10 | |
| **5. Postural Grounding** | Feet firmly planted shoulder-width apart; zero nervous swaying, pacing, or weight-shifting; moves only on transitions. | 10 | |
| **6. Kinesics & Gestures** | Hands operate inside the gestural box (collarbone to belt line); uses purposeful open-palm and precision gestures; zero fidgeting. | 10 | |
| **7. Eye Contact Anchoring** | Holds direct 3-to-5 second eye contact with individual audience anchors across all sectors; never sprays like a lighthouse. | 10 | |
| **8. Cognitive Structure** | Logical progression adheres strictly to an executive framework (Minto Pyramid, PREP, SCQA, or What/So What/Now What). | 10 | |
| **9. Audience Resonance & ELIF** | Translates complex technical abstractions into vivid real-world sensory analogies; connects technical metrics to business ROI. | 10 | |
| **10. Landing & Call to Action** | Delivers a full-circle callback to the opening hook; paints the transformed New Bliss; states an unambiguous call to action. | 10 | |
| **TOTAL SCORE** | **Out of 100 Possible Points** | **100** | |

---

## 2. Mastery Benchmarks & Scoring Bands

```text
  [ 90 – 100 POINTS ]: TIER 1 KEYNOTE / EXECUTIVE GRAVITAS
  • Broadcast-ready, commanding authority, zero filler words, flawless poise.
  • Capable of keynoting global conferences or presenting to Fortune 500 boards.

  [ 75 – 89 POINTS ]: TIER 2 PERSUASIVE PROFESSIONAL
  • Clear, confident, well-structured delivery; occasional minor pacing or vocal slips.
  • Highly effective in team reviews, sprint demos, and client meetings.

  [ 60 – 74 POINTS ]: TIER 3 FUNCTIONAL PRESENTER
  • Communicates basic technical facts; plagued by monotone delivery, slide-reading, or fidgeting.
  • Requires deliberate practice on vocal dials and slide anti-bullet rules.

  [ BELOW 60 POINTS ]: TIER 4 APPREHENSIVE NOVICE
  • Severe glossophobia, heavy filler words, rapid pacing, eye contact avoidance.
  • Focus immediately on Phase 0 somatic overrides and the daily 15-minute practice routine.
```
