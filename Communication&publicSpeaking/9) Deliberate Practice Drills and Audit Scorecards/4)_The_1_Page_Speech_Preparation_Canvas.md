# 4) The 1-Page Speech Preparation Canvas

## Executive Overview
When preparing for a keynote, board presentation, or technical all-hands meeting, opening PowerPoint or Google Slides as your first step is a fatal mistake. Drafting slides before defining your core message leads to bloated, 50-slide decks devoid of emotional resonance or clear structural progression. The **1-Page Speech Preparation Canvas** is an analog planning tool that forces structural rigor before you touch a slide tool.

---

## 1. The Speech Canvas Template

```text
================================================================================
                     1-PAGE SPEECH PREPARATION CANVAS
================================================================================

1. AUDIENCE PROFILE & STAKES
   • Who is in the room? (Roles, technical depth, power dynamics):
   • What is their primary unexpressed fear or burning problem?
   • What is the cost to them if this problem goes unsolved?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE CORE LOGLINE (THE ONE SENTENCE)
   If the audience forgets everything else 6 months from now, what is the single
   sentence they must remember?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE FIRST 60 SECONDS (THE HOOK)
   • Selected Archetype: [ ] Inquiry  [ ] Counter-Intuitive  [ ] Story  [ ] Metric
   • Word-for-Word Opening Hook Script:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE SPARKLINE CONTRAST (TENSION & RELEASE)
   • What Is (The painful, flawed current reality):
   • What Could Be (The transformed potential / future):

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE THREE STRUCTURAL PILLARS (LOGOS)
   • Pillar 1 (Proof / Metric):
   • Pillar 2 (Proof / Metric):
   • Pillar 3 (Proof / Metric):

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE SENSORY ANALOGY (ELIF TRANSLATION)
   What physical real-world metaphor explains the core complex technology?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE FINAL LANDING & CALL TO ACTION (CTA)
   • Full-Circle Callback to Hook:
   • The Vision of New Bliss:
   • The Explicit Action Required in Next 48 Hours:

================================================================================
```

---

## 2. Rules of the Canvas
- **Pen and Paper First**: Fill out this canvas by hand before creating a single digital slide.
- **The Logline Test**: If you cannot write your core thesis in one clean sentence of fewer than 20 words, your talk is unfocused.
- **The Slide Conversion**: Each box on this canvas directly maps to a section of your final presentation deck.
