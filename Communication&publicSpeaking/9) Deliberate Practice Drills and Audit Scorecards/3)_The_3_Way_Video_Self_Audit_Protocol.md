# 3) The 3-Way Video Self-Audit Protocol

## Executive Overview
Human self-perception during speaking is notoriously inaccurate due to cognitive overload. When you watch a recording of yourself normally (with both audio and video playing simultaneously), your brain becomes overwhelmed by vanity, self-consciousness, and cognitive bias. The **3-Way Video Self-Audit Protocol** separates sensory evaluation channels into three distinct passes, allowing you to objectively identify and eliminate physical, acoustic, and narrative flaws.

## 1. The 3-Pass Audit Engine

```text
               ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
               │          RECORD A 3-MINUTE SPEECH            │
               └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                      │
            ┌─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┐
            ▼                         ▼                         ▼
      [ PASS 1: VIDEO ONLY ]    [ PASS 2: AUDIO ONLY ]    [ PASS 3: COMBINED ]
      (Mute System Volume)      (Turn Off Monitor)        (Normal Playback)
      • Postural stability      • Filler word tally       • Cognitive structure
      • Foot swaying / pacing   • Pace / inflection       • Opening hook impact
      • Gestural box framing    • Pauses & vocal fry      • Call to Action power
```

---

## 2. Pass-by-Pass Evaluation Rubric

### Pass 1: Visual Kinesics (Mute Sound Completely)
Watch the video without hearing a single word. Score yourself:
- **Foot Anchoring**: Did my feet stay planted, or did I sway like a pendulum?
- **Gestural Box**: Did my hands operate between collarbone and belt line, or drop into pockets?
- **Facial Expression**: Did my face convey engagement and warmth, or did I wear a tense "terror scowl"?
- **Eye Anchoring**: Did my gaze hold for 3-second blocks, or dart nervously around the room?

### Pass 2: Acoustic Delivery (Turn Monitor Off / Screen Black)
Listen to the recording with closed eyes or headphones:
- **Filler Word Count**: Tally every single instance of *um, uh, like, you know, so yeah*. Target: Zero.
- **Downward Inflection**: Did my statements drop in pitch at the period, or rise like tentative questions?
- **Strategic Pausing**: Did I embrace pure silence before and after critical points, or rush through without breathing?
- **Vocal Resonance**: Did my voice project from the chest, or sound thin, nasally, and strained?

### Pass 3: Cognitive Flow & Persuasion (Normal Playback)
Watch and listen together:
- Did the hook grab attention in the first 15 seconds?
- Was the PREP or Minto structure easily identifiable?
- Was there a compelling, unambiguous call to action?
- Would I, as an executive or client, approve this proposal based on this delivery?
