# 6) Chapter 9 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **The 15-Minute Daily Workout**: Dedicate 15 minutes each morning to diaphragmatic sigh resets, lip trills, the 3-minute cork reading drill, a 60-second impromptu sprint, and an audio audit.
- **Table Topics Sprint Bank**: Draw from the 30-prompt question bank and use the 5-second mental assembly algorithm (Breathe $\longrightarrow$ Choose Framework $\longrightarrow$ Lock in Headline $\longrightarrow$ Deliver).
- **The 3-Way Video Self-Audit Protocol**: Deconstruct recordings across three distinct passes: Pass 1 (Video-only for body language), Pass 2 (Audio-only for vocalics and fillers), and Pass 3 (Combined for narrative logic).
- **The 1-Page Preparation Canvas**: Plan every speech on paper first before opening slide software. Define the audience fear, the core logline, the hook, and the call to action.
- **The 100-Point Evaluation Rubric**: Quantify speaking performance across 10 empirical dimensions to track progress from Functional Presenter (60–74) to Executive Gravitas (90–100).

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Daily Practice Habit**: I complete the 15-minute vocal and impromptu practice routine at least 4 mornings per week.
2. [ ] **Cork Drill Integration**: I use the wine cork drill before every major presentation or high-stakes meeting.
3. [ ] **5-Second Impromptu Reflex**: When handed a random prompt, I can assemble a structured PREP talk in under 5 seconds.
4. [ ] **Weekly Video Recording**: I record at least one 3-minute presentation or update video every week.
5. [ ] **Muted Video Audit**: I audit my recorded body language and foot grounding with the audio muted.
6. [ ] **Audio-Only Filler Tally**: I listen to my recorded audio with the monitor off and count every filler word.
7. [ ] **1-Page Canvas First**: I always map talks on the 1-Page Canvas before creating digital slides.
8. [ ] **Single Core Logline**: I can summarize any talk I give in a single sentence of fewer than 20 words.
9. [ ] **Rubric Tracking**: I grade my presentations using the 100-Point Rubric and track score improvements over time.
10. [ ] **Target Band Consistency**: My recorded presentations consistently score 85+ points on the Master Evaluation Rubric.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Unshakeable deliberate practice discipline; operating at the top 1% of technical communicators.
- **11–16**: Good practice routine; needs greater consistency with weekly video self-audits.
- **0–10**: Erratic or non-existent practice; commit to the 15-minute daily workout starting tomorrow morning.
