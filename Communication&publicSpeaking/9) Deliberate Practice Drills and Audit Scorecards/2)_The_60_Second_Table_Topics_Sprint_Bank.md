# 2) The 60-Second Table Topics Sprint Bank

## Executive Overview
Originating in Toastmasters International, **Table Topics** is the premier training drill for mastering impromptu speaking. The challenge: you are handed a surprise prompt, given only **5 seconds to think**, and must deliver a coherent, structured, persuasive 60-to-90-second speech without using filler words.

---

## 1. The 5-Second Mental Algorithm

```text
  [ PROMPT DELIVERED ]
           │
           ▼
  [ SECOND 1-2: SILENT BREATH & CHOOSE FRAMEWORK ]
  • Is this a decision/opinion? ──────────► USE PREP (Point, Reason, Example, Point)
  • Is this an event/incident? ───────────► USE WHAT? SO WHAT? NOW WHAT?
  • Is this a vision/evolution? ──────────► USE PAST, PRESENT, FUTURE
           │
           ▼
  [ SECOND 3-4: LOCK IN THE "POINT" (FIRST SENTENCE) ]
  • Formulate your declarative headline.
           │
           ▼
  [ SECOND 5: OPEN MOUTH & DELIVER ]
```

## 2. The 30 Technical & Leadership Practice Prompts

Use these prompts during your daily 15-minute workout:

### Group A: Technology & Architecture Decisions
1. *Should software engineering teams prioritize technical debt refactoring or new feature velocity?*
2. *Is AI going to replace junior software engineers by 2030?*
3. *Why do so many microservice migrations fail?*
4. *Should companies enforce strict return-to-office policies or remain fully remote?*
5. *What is more important in modern system design: high throughput or strict consistency?*
6. *Is automated unit testing overvalued compared to integration testing?*
7. *Why should non-technical CEOs care about cybersecurity?*
8. *What is the most overrated technology trend of the last five years?*
9. *Should developers be on-call for the code they deploy?*
10. *How do you know when a legacy monolith must finally be decommissioned?*

### Group B: Leadership, Conflict & Philosophy
11. *What makes an exceptional engineering manager?*
12. *How do you deliver bad news to an executive?*
13. *What is the difference between a Senior Engineer and a Staff Engineer?*
14. *How should an engineering team handle a major public production outage?*
15. *Can charisma be learned, or is it an innate gift?*
16. *Why is silence the most powerful tool in negotiation?*
17. *How do you rebuild trust after a team misses a critical company deadline?*
18. *What is the best way to handle a toxic high-performer?*
19. *Why do smart people struggle to communicate simply?*
20. *What does "disagree and commit" mean to you in practice?*

### Group C: Abstract & Creative Prompts (Brain Agility)
21. *If you could teach every human on earth one communication skill, what would it be?*
22. *Describe a coffee mug as if it were a high-tech distributed system.*
23. *Why do people fear public speaking more than financial bankruptcy?*
24. *If you had to explain the internet to someone from the 15th century, what would you say?*
25. *What is a mistake you made that taught you an invaluable life lesson?*
