# 6) Chapter 3 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **Minto Pyramid & BLUF**: Invert chronological thinking; state the recommendation in the very first sentence, followed by mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive (MECE) supporting pillars.
- **SCQA Architecture**: Hook technical and business audiences by setting a baseline (Situation), revealing the friction (Complication), asking the core dilemma (Question), and delivering the solution (Answer).
- **PREP Engine**: Use Point, Reason, Example, Point to answer 90% of impromptu business inquiries with zero preparation.
- **What? So What? Now What?**: The ultimate translation tool for incidents, status reports, and sprint demos. Always connect facts to business impact and next steps.
- **Tactical Stalling & Reframing**: Never rush into answering hostile or complex questions. Use pauses, clarifying questions, and dimensional chunking to buy 6–8 seconds of poise.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **BLUF Discipline**: I lead with my recommendation or decision in the first 15 seconds of speaking.
2. [ ] **SCQA Narrative Setup**: I can frame new technical proposals using Situation $\rightarrow$ Complication $\rightarrow$ Question $\rightarrow$ Answer.
3. [ ] **PREP Muscle Memory**: When asked an impromptu question, I instinctively structure my reply into Point $\rightarrow$ Reason $\rightarrow$ Example $\rightarrow$ Point.
4. [ ] **Example Quality**: My PREP examples feature concrete numbers, metrics, or past project precedents.
5. [ ] **"So What?" Verification**: I never report raw technical data without explaining its business or customer impact.
6. [ ] **"Now What?" Resolution**: I always conclude updates with a clear, measurable next step or owner.
7. [ ] **Intentional 3-Second Silence**: When asked a difficult question, I can pause silently for 3 seconds before answering.
8. [ ] **Reframing Hostile Inquiries**: I can rephrase loaded questions into constructive, objective architectural discussions.
9. [ ] **The Confident Deferral**: When I don't know a metric, I confidently state what I will look up and give a strict SLA.
10. [ ] **Brevity**: In meetings, I can deliver a full, persuasive argument in under 90 seconds without rambling.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Master of executive brevity, structural clarity, and impromptu influence.
- **11–16**: Strong cognitive structure; occasional tendency to get bogged down in methodology.
- **0–10**: Frequent rambling or unstructured responses; practice daily with 60-second PREP sprints.
