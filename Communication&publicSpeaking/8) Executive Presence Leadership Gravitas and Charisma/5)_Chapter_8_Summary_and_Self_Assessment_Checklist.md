# 5) Chapter 8 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **The Charisma Triad**: Cultivate the balance of **Presence** (undivided attention), **Power** (status, competence, physical stillness), and **Warmth** (benevolence and genuine goodwill).
- **Physical Stillness**: Eliminate bobblehead nodding, fidgeting, and restless foot movements. Claim your space at the table and project calm economy of motion.
- **Handling Interruptions**: Never shout or crumble when interrupted. Use the steamroller technique (maintain cadence and volume for 4 words) or the physical hand-stop with a warm boundary statement.
- **Strategic Silence**: Use the 2-second processing pause, master the negotiation silence (never negotiate against yourself), and command noisy rooms through stillness rather than shouting.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Undivided Presence**: I never look at my phone, watch, or secondary monitors while someone is speaking to me.
2. [ ] **Still Head Posture**: When listening in meetings, I maintain a still head and avoid rapid, submissive bobblehead nodding.
3. [ ] **Boardroom Table Claim**: In executive meetings, I take an active seat at the main table and claim my physical space.
4. [ ] **Economy of Motion**: When presenting, I move deliberately and eliminate nervous fidgeting with pens or clothing.
5. [ ] **Interruption Boundary**: When interrupted, I can calmly hold the floor or use the polite hand-stop without anger.
6. [ ] **Protecting Colleagues**: I step in to reclaim the floor for colleagues who have been spoken over or interrupted.
7. [ ] **The 2-Second Gravitas Pause**: When asked tough questions by leaders, I pause in silence for 2 seconds before speaking.
8. [ ] **Negotiation Silence**: After stating an ask or price, I stay completely quiet and let the other party speak first.
9. [ ] **No Self-Sabotaging Fluff**: I do not undermine my requests with trailing apologies or defensive over-justifications.
10. [ ] **Warmth & Power Balance**: I project both decisive competence and empathetic warmth without leaning into arrogance.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Elite executive presence and leadership gravitas.
- **11–16**: Strong commanding presence; occasional slips into over-justifying or nervous nodding under high pressure.
- **0–10**: Frequent deferential or nervous behaviors; practice physical stillness drills and the 2-second silence rule.
