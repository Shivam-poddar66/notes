# 6) Chapter 6 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **ELIF Translation Stack**: Bridge technical concepts to business leadership through Tangible Analogy $\longrightarrow$ Business/Financial Impact $\longrightarrow$ Technical Specifics.
- **RFC & ADR Advocacy**: Defend architecture through written documents with comprehensive "Alternatives Considered" matrices. Socialize proposals 1-on-1 with key skeptics before group review.
- **Constraint-Driven Framing**: Frame engineering disputes around objective trade-offs (Latency vs Throughput, Consistency vs Availability) and project dials (Scope, Cost, Timeline).
- **Managing Up**: Never bring problems without recommended solutions. Front-load risks early using the "No Surprises Rule", and speak in the currency of revenue, risk, and velocity.
- **High-Velocity Meetings & Blameless Post-Mortems**: Eliminate aimless meetings; enforce agendas and decision-makers. In post-mortems, attack systemic failure modes rather than individual humans.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Jargon Elimination**: I can explain complex distributed systems concepts without using acronyms or internal jargon.
2. [ ] **Financial & Risk Articulation**: When pitching tech improvements, I explicitly link them to revenue, cost, or downtime risk.
3. [ ] **RFC Completeness**: My design docs feature an exhaustive "Alternatives Considered" trade-off matrix.
4. [ ] **Socializing Decisions**: I discuss architectural proposals with key skeptics 1-on-1 prior to formal review meetings.
5. [ ] **Trade-Off Fluency**: I articulate architecture proposals as bundles of trade-offs rather than "the best" choice.
6. [ ] **Trilemma Negotiation**: When deadlines are compressed, I negotiate scope rather than making ungrounded promises.
7. [ ] **Solution-Oriented Updates**: I never present an obstacle to my manager without bringing at least one concrete recommendation.
8. [ ] **Early Risk Notification**: I inform leadership about slipping deadlines or impending bottlenecks days or weeks in advance.
9. [ ] **Meeting Facilitation**: I keep technical meetings under control, capping unproductive debates at 10 minutes.
10. [ ] **Blameless Post-Mortem Rigor**: During post-mortems, I focus on CI/CD guards and automated alerts rather than blaming engineers.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Elite technical communicator and trusted executive partner.
- **11–16**: Strong technical advocacy; occasional friction during intense cross-functional deadlines.
- **0–10**: Frequent misunderstandings with non-technical stakeholders; implement the 3-tier ELIF stack immediately.
