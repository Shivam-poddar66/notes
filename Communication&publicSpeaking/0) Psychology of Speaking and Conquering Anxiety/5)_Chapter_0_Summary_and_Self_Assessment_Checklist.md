# 5) Chapter 0 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **Neurobiology**: Stage fright is an evolutionary sympathetic nervous system reaction triggered by perceived social evaluation.
- **Somatic Overrides**: Use bottom-up biological hacks—specifically the **Huberman Double Physiological Sigh** and **Box Breathing**—to activate parasympathetic vagal braking before speaking.
- **Cognitive Framing**: Replace the ego-driven performance mindset (*"Will they judge me?"*) with the service mindset (*"How can I solve their problem?"*). Reframe acute anxiety as excitement.
- **Exposure Ladder**: Build long-term stress tolerance through daily micro-exposures, starting with private recordings and graduating to meeting participation within the first 5 minutes.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Physiological Recognition**: I recognize my physical anxiety triggers (heart rate, breath, voice tightening) without panicking.
2. [ ] **Pre-Speech Vagal Reset**: I execute 3 cycles of the Double Physiological Sigh before walking onto stage or unmuting.
3. [ ] **Box Breathing Competence**: I can regulate my breathing with a 4-4-4-4 rhythm during tense moments.
4. [ ] **Cognitive Reappraisal**: I verbalize *"I am excited"* rather than trying to suppress anxiety.
5. [ ] **Service Orientation**: I define the audience's primary takeaway before I begin speaking.
6. [ ] **Spotlight Effect Immunity**: I no longer obsess over minor verbal stumbles or misspoken syllables.
7. [ ] **Early Meeting Engagement**: I consistently speak or contribute within the first 5 minutes of group meetings.
8. [ ] **Asynchronous Video Fluency**: I comfortably record and send 1–2 minute video updates without anxiety or endless re-takes.
9. [ ] **Pre-Speech Hydration Protocol**: I hydrate with room-temperature water and avoid caffeine/sugar right before presentations.
10. [ ] **Postural Grounding**: I plant both feet and avoid nervous fidgeting, rocking, or pacing.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **16–20**: High autonomic resilience; fear converted to dynamic stage presence.
- **10–15**: Solid foundational control; occasional spikes during unexpected curveballs.
- **0–9**: High acute glossophobia; prioritize daily somatic drills and micro-exposure ladder.
