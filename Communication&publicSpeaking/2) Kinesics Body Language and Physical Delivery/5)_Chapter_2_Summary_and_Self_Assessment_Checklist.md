# 5) Chapter 2 Summary & Self-Assessment Checklist

## Chapter Summary
- **The Grounded Base**: Maintain shoulder-width feet with 50/50 balance and soft knees. Move across the stage only on transitions using the Anchor & Travel method.
- **The Gestural Box**: Keep hand movements framed between collarbone and belt line. Use the 4 master executive gestures (Open Book, Precision Pinch, Clean Chop, Expansive Globe).
- **The 3-Second Anchor Rule**: Break the audience into sectors; connect with one individual per full sentence rather than scanning like a lighthouse sprinkler.
- **Virtual Fluency**: Position camera at eye level, look into the lens, optimize front-facing key lighting, use a dedicated microphone, and deliver with +15% energy to pierce screen compression.

---

## 10-Point Self-Assessment Checklist

Score each item from **0 (Never)** to **2 (Consistently Mastered)**:

1. [ ] **Shoulder-Width Base**: My feet are positioned under my shoulders without crossing or excessive width.
2. [ ] **Zero Swaying / Rocking**: I deliver points in complete physical stillness without nervous heel-to-toe rocking.
3. [ ] **Semantic Stage Movement**: When I walk on stage, it is intentional, forward/diagonal, and connects with transitions.
4. [ ] **Gestural Box Framing**: My hands remain between my belt and collarbone during formal delivery.
5. [ ] **No Pocket Hiding**: My hands are visible, open, and never hidden in pockets or behind my back.
6. [ ] **Elimination of Fidgeting**: I do not click pens, spin rings, touch my face, or play with clickers while speaking.
7. [ ] **3-to-5 Second Eye Anchoring**: I maintain continuous eye contact with one person for a full sentence before shifting.
8. [ ] **Sector Coverage**: In a presentation room, I distribute eye contact evenly across left, center, and right sectors.
9. [ ] **Eye-Level Remote Camera**: My webcam is elevated directly to my eye line, preventing unflattering downward angles.
10. [ ] **Lens Focus on Camera**: In virtual meetings, I look into the camera lens when delivering key points rather than at video tiles.

**Scoring Guide**:
- **17–20**: Flawless physical stage presence and commanding kinesics.
- **11–16**: Solid physical delivery; minor nervous movement under high stress.
- **0–10**: Heavy physical leakage of anxiety (swaying, hidden hands, erratic eye contact); practice with muted video recording audits.
